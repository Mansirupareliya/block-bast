2. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and choices, these ads may be personalized. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

3. Third-party services

We do not sell your information.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To have your leaderboard and streak entries deleted from our servers, email us with the player ID shown in the app's Settings screen, and we will delete them within 30 days.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but the app only stores game data, never sensitive personal information.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
